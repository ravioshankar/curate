# Privacy Policy for iQRate

**Effective Date:** September 24, 2025

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how iQRate ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
iQRate does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. All data you enter into the app is stored locally on your device.

### Data You Provide
- Collection item details (names, descriptions, categories, locations)
- Photos of your personal belongings
- Valuation information
- Personal notes and comments
- App settings and preferences

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information:** We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or other technical information
- **Usage Analytics:** We do not track app usage or user behavior
- **Location Data:** We do not access or collect location information

## How We Use Information

All information you provide is used solely to:
- Display your personal collection within the app
- Provide search and organization features
- Store your preferences and settings
- Enable app functionality on your device

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage Only:** All your data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms
- **No Cloud Sync:** We do not upload, sync, or backup your data to any external servers
- **Device Security:** Your data security depends on your device's security settings
- **No Third-Party Access:** We do not share your data with any third parties

## Data Sharing

We do **not** share, sell, rent, or disclose any of your information to third parties because:
- We do not collect personal information
- All data remains on your device
- We have no access to your data

## Children's Privacy

iQRate does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since all data is stored locally on the device and we do not collect any information, the app can be used by children under parental supervision.

## Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your information:
- **Access:** View all your data within the app
- **Modify:** Edit or update any information
- **Delete:** Remove individual items or clear all data
- **Export:** Data remains accessible through the app interface

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and will be effective immediately upon posting.

## Third-Party Services

iQRate does not integrate with any third-party services that collect personal information.
